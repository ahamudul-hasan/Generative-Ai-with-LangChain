### **The Beautiful Game**

Under the lights, the stadium roars,
Dreams are born on grassy floors.
A whistle blows, the battle starts,
Twenty-two players, beating hearts.

The ball rolls fast, the crowd goes wild,
Every pass is fierce and every run is styled.
A perfect cross, a flying head—
One magic moment, and tears are shed.

Through rain and sweat, through pain and pride,
You fight for those standing by your side.
Ninety minutes, one goal in sight,
You never give up, you always fight.

For some, it's just a game they play,
For others, it's life every day.
And when the final whistle calls,
The love of football never falls.

From dusty fields to stadium lights,
From childhood dreams to Friday nights,
One ball, one goal, one endless dream—
Football is more than it may seem. ⚽
